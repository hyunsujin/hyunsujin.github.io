Research Publication

Stress Prediction Using Personalized Pre-Sleep Lifelog Features

Conference
ICTC 2026
Format
Poster Presentation
Authors
Kyeongran Moon · Sujin Hyun
Context
DACON × ETRI Lifelog Dataset / Paper Competition

Hongik University — Electronics and Electrical EngineeringKAIST — Culture Technology, IBD Lab

Overview

This study predicts pre-sleep stress (Q3) from smartphone and wearable lifelog data in the DACON/ETRI Lifelog Dataset 2024. It combines raw behavioral and environmental features from the 30 minutes before bedtime with personalized Z-score features, which read each signal relative to a person's own baseline, and recent questionnaire and sleep history. Regularized logistic regression was evaluated with nested chronological and nested leave-one-user-out validation.
